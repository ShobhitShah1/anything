Microphone & speech recognition

Used when you tap the microphone to dictate a draft. Your device may ask for microphone and speech-recognition access. The recognition service may process audio remotely. Denying access leaves typed capture available; dictation needs the permission.

Photos & files

Used to select an image, import a backup or export a collection. Availability and the scope of photo access depend on the operating system and picker. The app does not need camera permission for its current image-picker flow. Choose only the files you want to save or import.

Clipboard

Used for paste/save and optional clipboard capture while the app is active. Clipboard text can contain sensitive information: leave automatic capture off unless you want it. The desktop extension has separate optional permissions and captures copied content only when its feature is enabled. Password fields are excluded by the extension, but clipboard capture cannot guarantee detection of every secret.

Network & sign-in

Network access loads link previews and remote images and enables optional sync, AI and billing. Google sign-in establishes your Firebase account. It does not grant the app general access to your Gmail or Drive. Local saves and typed notes can be used without signing in; online features require a connection.

Change your mind

Manage app access from your operating system's Settings → Apps → Anything → Permissions, or use the browser's site settings. Names vary by device. Turning off a permission stops the related feature; it does not automatically erase information already saved or sent to a provider.

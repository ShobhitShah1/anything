Account & Data Deletion

Export your collection, remove saves, and understand what account deletion requires.

1. Export first

In the mobile app, open Settings → Export collection. On the web, use the export control in Settings. Keep the JSON backup somewhere safe. A local-only collection can be lost after uninstalling or clearing site data.

2. Remove your saves

Use Settings → Delete all saves, or remove individual saves. If cloud sync is enabled, the corresponding collection changes are synced to that account. This removes saved content; it is not a complete account deletion. Signing out only ends your session.

3. Request account and associated-data deletion

The current app does not include an automated account-deletion control. The operator must provide and test an in-app request route plus an external request method before a store release that requires them. Until those are operational, this page must not be presented as a completed deletion service.

Once the operator enables requests below, send a request from the email associated with your Anything account. Do not send a password, payment-card number, ID document or private key. The operator may need to verify account ownership before deleting Firebase identity and associated cloud records. Subscription and provider records may have separate retention obligations.

4. Cancel a subscription separately

Manage or cancel an active subscription through Google Play or Apple. Account deletion or uninstalling does not cancel store billing. Purchase history and records required by the store or applicable law may remain with those providers.

Account-deletion requests are not enabled yet. The operator must configure and test the request channel before this page can be used for a store release.
